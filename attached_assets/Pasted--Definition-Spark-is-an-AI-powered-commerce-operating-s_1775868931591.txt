
Definition:
Spark is an AI-powered commerce operating system that takes a user from idea → product → content → sales → profit → scale.

⸻

🧠 1. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE (HIGH LEVEL)

Spark is made of 6 interconnected engines:
	1.	Intelligence Engine (what to sell)
	2.	Offer Engine (how to sell it)
	3.	Creation Engine (assets generation)
	4.	Distribution Engine (traffic)
	5.	Commerce Engine (sales + delivery)
	6.	Optimization Engine (improvement + scaling)

👉 Most apps only do 1–2. You’re doing all 6.
